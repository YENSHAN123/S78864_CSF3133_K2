About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began as the Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, providing facilities for both students and lecturers from the Fisheries and Marine Science programmes to conduct practical sessions as well as research.

Eventually, the Universiti Pertanian Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT) branch campus was established in June 1996. On 1 May 2001, it was granted autonomy as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) before being officially upgraded to a full-fledged university and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu on 1 February 2007.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier marine-focused university, specializing in marine science, aquatic resources, environmental conservation, and maritime studies, while also offering diverse programs in business, social development, and computer science.
